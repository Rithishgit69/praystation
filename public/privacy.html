Privacy

The Temple of Eka-Danta (the "Game") collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no passwords, no payments, no advertisements, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party services.

  • The name you give your traveller, your chosen traveller, your settings and your save game are stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never transmitted anywhere and can be cleared at any time from the title screen ("New game") or by clearing site data.
  • After the page has loaded, the Game makes no network requests except to fetch its own static files (narration clips and optional villain models) from the same site it was loaded from. Installed as a web app it plays fully offline.
  • No scores or results are sent to any server; there is no leaderboard.

Effective 21 September 2026.
