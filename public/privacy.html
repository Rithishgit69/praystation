Privacy

The Temple of Eka-Danta (the "Game") collects no personal data beyond the name you choose to type. There are no accounts, no passwords, no payments, no advertisements, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party services.

  • The name you give your traveller, your chosen traveller, your settings and your save game are stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never transmitted anywhere and can be cleared at any time from the title screen ("New game") or by clearing site data.
  • After the page has loaded, the Game makes no network requests except to fetch its own static files (narration clips, the leaderboard configuration and optional villain models) from the same site it was loaded from, and the leaderboard requests described below. Installed as a web app it plays fully offline.
  • Finished runs (the traveller's chosen name, rank, battle time, accuracy and hearts lost) are recorded in your browser's local storage for the on-device leaderboard. Only if the site's operator has configured a shared leaderboard (a leaderboard.json file next to the Game) is that same record sent, once, to the operator's own database when a run is completed, so it can be listed on the "Everyone" board. Nothing else is sent; the name is whatever you typed and need not identify you.

Effective 22 September 2026.
