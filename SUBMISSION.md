## How to play (short note)

**Goal.** Eight tasks. Each task introduces one asura of the Vinayaka Purana tradition with a narrated
card, then grants you the Astra — a gun of remembered light. Empty the villain's health bar (top of the
screen) to complete the task and move to the next; defeat all eight to restore the temple and see your
rank. You have three hearts: when an asura brings you down you lose one and return to the edge of the
arena; lose all three and you can continue from the same stage or replay the previous task.

**Controls (keyboard + mouse).** Move `W A S D` · look with the mouse (click the view to capture it,
`Esc` releases) · run `Shift` · jump `Space` · dodge `Q` · fire `left mouse` · aim `right mouse` ·
reload `R` · pause / options / choose a task `Esc`. Gamepad and touch are supported (right stick / swipe
to look, `RT` / FIRE button to fire).

**Tips.** Every projectile flies straight — step sideways, never straight back. Every floor effect is
marked before it hurts — leave the circle. Each villain has its own style (fire, sword, chain, mace,
bow, roots, mirror); the card and the tip on the Astra tell you how to beat it.
