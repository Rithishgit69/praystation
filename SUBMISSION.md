## How to play (short note)

**Goal.** Five tasks. Each task introduces one asura of the Vinayaka Purana tradition with a narrated
card, then the temple grants you a weapon of remembered light — the Astra rifle in Task 1, the Dhanush
bow in Task 2, the Chakra disc in Task 3, the Vajra burst in Task 4; all of them stay with you. Empty the
villain's health bar (top of the screen) to complete the task and move to the next; defeat all five to
restore the temple, see your rank and enter the leaderboard. Each villain is harder than the last and
the fifth is the real test. You have three hearts: when an asura brings you down you lose one and
return to the edge of the arena; lose all three and you can continue from the same stage or replay the
previous task.

**Controls (keyboard + mouse).** Move `W A S D` · look with the mouse (click the view to capture it,
`Esc` releases) · run `Shift` · jump `Space` · dodge `Q` (a quick roll the way you are moving) · fire
`left mouse` · aim `right mouse` · switch weapon `1 2 3 4` or the mouse wheel · reload `R` · **`B` shows
the controls at any time and pauses the game** · pause / options / leaderboard / choose a task `Esc`.
Nothing else — there is no crouch or stance to get stuck in. Gamepad and touch are supported (right
stick / swipe to look, `RT` / FIRE to fire, D-pad / ⟳ to switch, Back / ? for the controls).

**Tips.** Every projectile flies straight — step sideways, never straight back. Every floor effect is
marked before it hurts — leave the circle. Each villain has its own style (the duelling blade, the
wrestler's leap and flurry, buffalo horns and the bellow, six arms of shards and illusions, fire breath
and the mirror); the card and the tip on each weapon tell you how to beat it. A full bow draw pierces
shields' gaps and hits the core hardest; the Vajra staggers up close; the Chakra cuts twice per throw.

## Rules checklist

- A game, playable start → result → restart (title → traveller → how to play → 5 tasks → rank card + leaderboard → new game / return to title).
- Works at the live link without accounts or permissions. No passwords, payments or analytics; the only data kept is what the leaderboard shows — the traveller's chosen name and the run's rank, time, accuracy and hearts lost — stored in the browser (and, if the shared board is configured, on the team's own Supabase project; `docs/LEADERBOARD.md`, `public/privacy.html`).
- Leaderboard integrity: runs are recorded by the game itself only when the fifth task is completed; there is no score entry, no editing and no deletion; the shared board's policies allow only inserts and reads and its checks reject out-of-range rows.
- Own work; libraries listed above; AI assistance disclosed; every asset licensed (`docs/CREDITS.md`).
- Cultural care: inspired by traditional stories, fictional events; sacred figures are portrayed with respect and never fought; villains are the tradition's asuras. Disclaimer on the title screen and in the pause menu.
