**Full description**

The Temple of Eka-Danta is a cinematic mythological adventure where a player explores an abandoned
temple under moonlight. Every mural and relic can awaken a playable memory inspired by traditional
stories of Ganesha. Solve ancient puzzles, survive supernatural encounters and uncover why the temple
was built to remember — and what is trying to make it forget.

Eight tasks stand between you and the sanctum. Each opens with the name and the danger of an asura
from the Vinayaka Purana tradition — envy, pride, delusion, greed, anger, desire, attachment, ego —
narrated in a voice that means it. The temple grants you the Astra, a gun of remembered light. Three
hearts. A villain that gets stronger with every task. When you fall, choose: replay the last task, or
carry on from the same stage.

• A seamless 1.2 km temple world with no loading screens
• Eight escalating boss battles with distinct attack patterns
• Story mode: memories, moonlight puzzles, a serpent chase, a manuscript library
• Keyboard, gamepad and one-thumb touch controls
• Fully offline; no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no tracking

*Inspired by traditional stories; all events, characters and the temple in this game are fictional.*
