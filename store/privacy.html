Privacy Policy

The Temple of Eka-Danta (the "Game") does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

  • Save games and settings are stored only on your device (browser local storage or the app's private storage) and never leave it.
  • The Game makes no network requests after installation; it plays fully offline.
  • There are no accounts, advertisements, in-app purchases, analytics or third-party SDKs.
  • An optional "share anonymous crash reports" toggle exists in Options. It is off by default, and in this version no reporting service is bundled, so enabling it sends nothing.
  • The Game does not knowingly collect information from children; it collects no information from anyone.
